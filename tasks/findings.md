Dispositions may be `open`, `scheduled`, `deferred`, `accepted_risk`, or `resolved`. Include the decision reference, residual risk, and revisit condition for a deferral or accepted risk. A deferred check remains unperformed; a failed check remains failed until new evidence changes it.

Recommend relevant findings before implementation. Carry forward valid decisions without requesting the same approval again. Revisit when scope, impact, evidence, or the agreed trigger changes. Critical findings require immediate escalation and a pause of affected unsafe steps; independent authorized work may continue.
